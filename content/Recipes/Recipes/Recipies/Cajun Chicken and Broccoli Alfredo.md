##### Ingredients #####
- 1.5lbs boneless skinless chicken
	- Chopped small
- 3tbsp [[Cajun Seasoning]]
- 1tbsp olive oil
- 1 red bell
- 1 yellow bell
- 1 orange bell
- 2 cup broccoli
- 1/2 large onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 8oz protein pasta
- 2 cups fat free half and half 
	- sub coconut milk
- 2.5 cups low sodium chicken broth
- 1-2 tsp cajun seasoning
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste
- 1 med zucchini
- 1/2 cup parm - 56g

##### Instructions
1. Chop chicken and season with cajun seasoning
2. In dutch oven heat oil on med-high. Cook chicken until cooked through. Approx 5mins then remove
3. Add veg onion and garlic and saute until soft
4. Add liquids and cajun seasoning. Salt and pepper to taste. Bring to boil
5. Add pasta. Cover and reduce the heat. Cook for about 10-15 mins
6. Add zucchini and parm let simmer for 3-5 mins
7. Add back chicken. Let simmer for 3-5 mins
8. Turn off heat for sauce to thicken then serve

##### Macros
6 Servings - 410g each
Protein - 36g
Fat - 9g
Carbs - 56g
Fiber - 7 g
