##### Ingredients
- 6 kodiak cakes buttermilk and vaniila power waffles 
	- Cooked and chopped
- 15 pieces of air fry crispy chicken
	- Any frozen chicken works
- Pinch of salt
- pepper
- 1/2 cup low sugar syrup - 120ml
- 2Tbsp honey - 42g
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 cup liquid egg white - 100g
- 1.5 cups low fat milk - 360ml
- 1/4 cup mild cheddar - 28g
- 1/4 cup monterey jack 
- 1 green onion
- chives

##### Instructions
1. Heat oven to 375. Grease 9x13 pan 
2. Microwave chicken 1-2mins
	1. Thawed enough to chop
3. Toast waffles and chop 
	1. Approx 16 squares
4. In bowl combine waffles and chicken. Drizzle 0 sugar syrup and honey. Add salt and pepper then toss to coat and add to baking dish
5. In another bowl whisk eggs and milk. Add cheese green onion and chives
6. Pour egg mix evenly over waffles and chicken
7. Bake for 45-50mins 
8. Remove and let rest for 5-10 mins

##### Macros
Servings 6
Protein - 25g
Fat - 15g
Carbs - 40g
Fiber - 2g
