##### Ingredients
- 1lb boneless skinless chicken
###### Bowl
- 10oz bag frozen riced cauliflower
- Basmati rice
- 2 green onion
- Edamame
###### Garnish
- Chopped green onion
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Red pepper flakes

##### Instructions
1. Preheat airfryer - 380f
2. Chop chicken into small pieces
3. Fry chicken about 10mins
4. Add chicken to [[ Teriyaki Sauce]]
5. Build bowl
	1. 120g rice 
	2. 120g chicken
	3. Vegetables of choice
