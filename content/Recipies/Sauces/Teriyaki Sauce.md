##### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Coconutt Aminos - 160g
- 1tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tbsp 0 sugar maple syrup
- 1tsp garlic powder
- 1/4tsp ground ginger
- 1tsp cornstarch
- 1tsp water

##### Instructions
1. Add all ingredients to a pan over medium heat besides sturry
2. When simmering add slurry and mix well until thickened
