##### Ingredients
- 4 ears of corn
- 1.5 cups low fat milk
- 3 cloves garlic
- Fresh thyme - 1 sprig
- 3 cups chopped tomatoes
	- Small little cherries probably best
- 3tbsp chopped  fresh basil
- 2tbsp olive oil
- salt 
- 1tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup shallot
- 2tbsp flour
- 2tbsp grated parm
- pepper
- 1.25lbs halibut or other white fish
	- Divide into 4 pieces

##### Instructions
1. Cut kernels from cobs and set aside. 
2. Cut or break the cobs in half and place in a large saucepan. Add milk, 2 garlic cloves and thyme. Cook over medium heat until starting to simmer around the edges. Remove from heat, cover and let steep for 10 minutes. 
3. Strain into a glass measuring cup or small bowl; discard the solids. 
4. Grate remaining garlic in bowl stir in tomatoes, basil, 1tbsp oil and pinch of salt. Set aside
5. Melt butter in pan over medium. Add shallots and cook until soft. 
6. Add corn kernels about 3 mins. 
7. Sprinkle in flour and cook quick for 30sec.
8. Slowly add milk and while stirring. Maintain a simmer, cook until thickened
9. Add cheese and salt pepper. Cover and set aside
10. Salt and pepper fish.
11. Heat oil in large pan, med-high. Cook fish about 5-7mins until lightly browned
12. Serve over creamed corn and tomatoes
