These incredibly easy cookies require just 6 ingredients and one bowl! They are gluten-free, flourless, and naturally vegan.

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 12 cookies
## Ingredients

- 1 cup (250g) creamy peanut butter, unsweetened & unsalted
- ½ cup (100g) brown sugar, lightly packed
- ⅓ cup (65g) granulated sugar
- ¼ cup (60ml) plant-based milk (e.g., almond, soy, oat)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp baking soda
- A pinch of salt (optional, omit if your peanut butter is salted)
- Coarse sugar, for rolling (optional)
## Instructions

1. **Preheat Oven:** Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. **Mix Wet Ingredients:** In a large mixing bowl, add the peanut butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar, plant-based milk, and vanilla extract. Mix well with a spatula until a smooth, thick batter forms.
3. **Add Dry Ingredients:** Add the baking soda and (optional) salt to the bowl. Mix again until everything is fully incorporated and no streaks of baking soda remain.
4. **Chill (Optional but Recommended):** For thicker cookies that spread less, chill the dough for 15-30 minutes.
5. **Shape Cookies:** Scoop out about 1.5 tablespoons of dough per cookie. Roll the dough into balls between your palms. If desired, roll the balls in coarse sugar for a crunchy exterior.
6. **Create Criss-Cross Pattern:** Place the dough balls onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart. Use a fork to gently press down on each ball, creating a classic criss-cross pattern.
7. **Bake:** Bake for 9-11 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden. The centers will still look soft and slightly underbaked.
8. **Cool:** Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely. They will firm up as they cool.

## Notes

- **Peanut Butter:** For best results, use a natural, drippy peanut butter. Avoid using peanut butter that is overly thick or hard.
- **Sugar:** The cookies are moderately sweet. For a less sweet cookie, you can reduce the total sugar by 2-3 tablespoons.
