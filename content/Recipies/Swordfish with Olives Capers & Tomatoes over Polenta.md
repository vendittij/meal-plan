##### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup polenta
- 1tbsp olive oil
- 4 celery stalks - diced
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 15oz diced tomatoes
- 1/4 cup green olices - rinsed pitted and chopped
	- Sicilian colossal
	- Cerignola
- 3tbsp chopped fresh basil
- 1tbsp capers
- pepper 
- salt
- pinch crushed red pepper
- 1lb swordfish - cut into 4 steaks
- Fresh basil

##### Instructions
1. Bring 2 cups of water to a boil. Add salt. Slowly pour in polenta. Stirring with whisk or spoon. Cook for until thickens constantly stirring
2. Reduce heat to low simmer. Stir every 5 mins for ~20/25mins
	1. Stir out/crush any lumps
3. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm
4. Heat oil in large skillet over medium heat
5. Add celery and cook until tender. Add garlic, cook 30sec. Stir in tomatoes, olives, basil, capers, black pepper, red pepper, and salt to taste. Cover, reduce heat to low and cook for 5 mins
6. Place swordfish in simmering sauce. Cook until cooked through ~10/15mins
7. Serve fish over polenta, top with sauce and fresh basil
