##### Ingredients
- 1lb ground meat
- 1/2 cup shredded carrot
- 1/2 head broccoli, chopped - 115g
- 3oz snow peas
- 8oz whole water chestnuts
- 2 green onions
- Salt and Pepper to taste
- [[Stirfry Sauce|Stirfry Sauce]]

##### Instructions
1. Brown chicken over medium heat
2. Prep [[Stirfry Sauce]]
3. Chop broccoli microwave with a little water in a bowl  to soften
4. Add carrots and broccoli to meat. Cook for 3-5 mins
5. Add stirfry sauce, water chestnuts, green onions and snow peas. Simmer for 5mins
6. Thicken sauce with cornstarch slurry
7. Serve over cauliflower rice/rice/noodles
