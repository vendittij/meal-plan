#### **Ingredients**
- 1 lb mild Italian sausage (or your favorite variety)
- 8 large eggs
- 1/4 cup milk (or heavy cream for a richer taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh chives or green onions (optional for garnish)
#### **Instructions**
1. **Preheat Oven:** Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 12-cup muffin tin well with non-stick cooking spray or line with parchment paper liners.
2. **Cook Sausage:** In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook the sausage, breaking it up with a spoon, until it is browned and cooked through. Drain off any excess grease.
3. **Whisk Eggs:** In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper together until well combined and slightly frothy.
4. **Combine Mixture:** Add the cooked sausage and shredded cheddar cheese to the egg mixture. Stir until everything is evenly distributed.
5. **Fill Muffin Cups:** Pour the egg and sausage mixture evenly into the prepared muffin cups, filling each about 3/4 full.
6. **Bake:** Place in the preheated oven and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the tops are lightly golden and the centers are set (a toothpick inserted should come out clean).
7. **Cool and Serve:** Allow the muffins to cool in the pan for 5 minutes before removing. Garnish with fresh chives or green onions if desired. Serve warm.
#### **Notes**
- **Storage:** Let the muffins cool completely before storing. Keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
- **Freezing:** These muffins freeze beautifully. Place cooled muffins in a single layer on a baking sheet to freeze solid, then transfer to a freezer bag. They will keep for up to 3 months. Reheat in the microwave or a 350°F oven until warm.
- **Customization:** Feel free to add other ingredients like diced bell peppers, spinach, or mushrooms. Just be sure to sauté any watery vegetables first to remove excess moisture.
